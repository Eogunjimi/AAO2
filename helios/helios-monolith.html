( 04 — Process )

From audit
to autonomy.

A four-step method refined across 4,800 installations. No surprises, no spaghetti cabling, no vendor lock-in — just engineered sunshine.

/ 01

Audit

Load profiling, shading studies and a full solar simulation of your site. We measure your energy life before we touch it.

/ 02

Design

Engineering drawings, 3D renders and yield modelling. You approve a system you can already see producing.

/ 03

Install

Certified crews, torque-logged racking, labelled switchgear. Average residential install: five days, broom-clean handover.
